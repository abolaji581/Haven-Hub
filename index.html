Our Story

Haven Hub began as a simple idea: a place where a good meal and a good night's sleep didn't have to happen in two different parts of town. Right here in Egbeda, what started as a small dining room has grown into a home for travelers and locals alike.

A restaurant, a hotel, and a handful of quiet spaces in between — all built around one idea: hospitality done properly.
